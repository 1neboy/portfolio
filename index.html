01 — About

End-to-end ownership, from schema to screen.

I'm a full stack developer working at the intersection of banking infrastructure and product engineering. My day-to-day spans database design and query tuning on one end, and Angular interfaces that operations teams actually rely on at the other.

I've delivered reconciliation engines, dispute and chargeback workflows, EMI platforms and terminal management systems — the unglamorous, high-stakes systems where correctness and security aren't optional. That means OAuth 2.0 and JWT on the edge, AES-256-CBC with per-transaction salt and IV for sensitive data, and clean, well-documented APIs that partners can integrate against without hand-holding.

I also like the human side of delivery: running client demos, walking partners through UAT, and leading a team when timelines get tight. I led a five-developer squad through a critical transition and shipped every open project on schedule.
